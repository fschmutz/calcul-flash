# FAQ

**Is this for school?** It is a family arcade, age-calibrated 8–14. Not a curriculum and not a grade.

**Why Paloma?** Default first name. Change it; the last player is remembered on this device.

**Leaderboard?** No. Scores stay in the browser. There is no backend.

**Can I play offline?** After the first visit, the service worker caches the shell. `version.json` is fetched no-store when online so you can notice a new build.

**EN / FR?** French default. Toggle in the marquee. Saved as `calcul-flash-lang`.
